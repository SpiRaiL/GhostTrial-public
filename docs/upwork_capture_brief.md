# Upwork job post — motion reference capture (spear throw → uppercut)

**Targeted at dancers / choreographers, not martial artists.** Mocap performers
barely exist on Upwork; dancers and movement artists are everywhere, they already
film themselves to a brief, and they are trained in precisely what our authored clip
lacks — dynamics, weight transfer, level changes, sustained holds and repeatable
timing. The move is describable as a two-part phrase, so no combat background is
needed.

**Attach `reference_phrase.mp4`** (report folder, built by
`tools/make_reference_cut.py`, 2.2 MB, under any Upwork limit). The post references
the attachment only — no YouTube links, since applicants often won't click out, and
the timestamps into our own cut are far more precise than "watch 2:00-2:06 of this".
It is cut as ONE continuous phrase with a crossfade at the seam, the second window's
2.4 s idle head trimmed so the descent follows the throw's recovery without a dead
stop, and the source watermark removed.

Only ask for what **cannot be fixed in software**. Background removal, trimming,
cropping, colour, format conversion and mirroring are all done in-repo.

Angles rather than a mirrored take: mirroring is an exact geometric reflection we
already do for free; camera angle changes how much depth the monocular 3D solve has
to guess. ~45° is the sweet spot for this phrase, so most reps go there.

## Title

**Dancer / movement artist — learn and film a 6-second movement phrase for a hobby robotics project (~1 hour, your own space)**

## The job

Hobby project: I'm teaching a simulated humanoid robot a short movement phrase, and I
need clean video of a trained mover performing it so I can convert it into 3D motion
data.

One phrase, a few reps, from three camera positions. Your space, your camera, about
an hour. I do all the processing — I just need the movement on video.

## The phrase

**Everything you need is in the attached video** (29 s, no sound). It plays the whole
phrase through once at normal speed, then again at half speed. Watch the slow pass —
that's the one to learn it from.

| in the video | |
|---|---|
| 0:00–0:06 | **the throw** — from a wide, grounded stance: wind the torso back, then a sharp accent forward. One arm shoots straight out at shoulder height and **holds** at full extension. |
| 0:06–0:09 | **the rise** — release straight down into a deep crouch, close to the floor, then an explosive rise driving the same arm up overhead. Settle. |
| 0:09–0:29 | the same phrase again at half speed |

**Perform it as one unbroken phrase**, roughly 12–15 seconds at your own pacing.
There's a short dissolve around 0:06 where my reference is stitched from two takes —
ignore it, don't reproduce it as a pause. The release from the throw should feed
straight into the drop.

What I'm after is the *quality*, not a literal copy of the person in the video:
committed weight, clean lines, a real contrast between the slow wind-up and the sharp
accent, and a genuinely sustained hold at full extension. The transition between the
two parts matters as much as either part.

The reference is old, low-resolution footage of a stage rehearsal — don't worry about
matching the costume, the setting or the character. I only need the movement.

## What I need

**Three or four reps of the full phrase, then move the camera and repeat.** Three
positions:

1. **About 45° to the direction you reach** — the main one, most reps here
2. **Side on**, square to the reach
3. **Front on**, facing you

Same performance each time. If you have time, one slower rep and one sharper rep in
the 45° set are useful. Start and end every take standing still for a couple of
seconds — don't trim them off.

Five things I can't fix at my end, so they have to be right in camera:

- **Your whole body in frame the entire time** — feet never cropped, including at the bottom of the crouch
- **Camera stabilisation off** (it warps the image and breaks the 3D solve)
- **60 fps or better**, 1080p minimum, landscape
- **Enough light for a sharp image** — the fast accents shouldn't blur
- **Fitted dancewear** — leggings/fitted top. No baggy trousers, flowing skirts or loose layers; the software fits a body to your silhouette, and loose fabric becomes the body.

Don't worry about background, framing or tidying anything up — I handle all of that.
Barefoot or plain trainers, whichever you'd rather work in.

**Send the original camera files, unedited.** No trimming, grading, filters or
re-encoding. Drive/Dropbox/WeTransfer is fine.

If something's technically off in the first batch I may ask for one quick redo.

Just so it's clear up front: the finished robot animation goes in a public hobby
project write-up and video. Happy to credit you.

## To apply

Your dance/movement background, what you'll film on and at what frame rate, and when
you could deliver. A short clip of you moving is welcome but not required.
